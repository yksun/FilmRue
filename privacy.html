FilmRue Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026

FilmRue is made by Yukun Sun. In short: FilmRue collects no data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it makes no network requests.

Camera and microphone

The camera is used to show the viewfinder and take photos and videos. The microphone records sound only while you record a video. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Photos

FilmRue saves the photos and videos you take to your Photos library, and opens photos you choose so you can edit them. The original is kept inside each edited photo so you can revert it. All processing happens on your iPhone.

Location

Off by default. If you turn on Save Location, the place where a photo or video was taken is written into that file in your Photos library, as the Camera app does. FilmRue does not store or send your location anywhere else.

Settings

Your settings and saved films stay on your iPhone. Recipes you share are files you send yourself.

Children

FilmRue collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

FilmRue 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 9 日

FilmRue 由孙宇坤开发。简而言之：FilmRue 不收集任何数据。没有账户、广告、统计或追踪，也不进行任何网络请求。

相机与麦克风

相机用于显示取景器、拍摄照片和视频。麦克风只在录制视频时录音。任何内容都不会发送出去。

照片

FilmRue 把拍摄的照片和视频保存到“照片”图库，并打开你选择的照片进行编辑。原图保存在每张编辑过的照片里，可以随时还原。所有处理都在你的 iPhone 上完成。

位置

默认关闭。打开“记录拍摄位置”后，拍摄地点会像系统相机一样写入“照片”图库中的该文件。FilmRue 不会在其他地方保存或发送你的位置。

设置

你的设置和保存的胶片只保存在 iPhone 上。分享的配方是你自己发送的文件。

儿童

FilmRue 不收集任何人（包括儿童）的个人信息。

变更

如本政策有变更，新版本会发布在本页并注明新日期。
