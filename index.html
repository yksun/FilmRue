FilmRue · 胶片相机

A film camera for iPhone and iPhone Duo. See the film look live in the viewfinder; every photo is rendered again at full resolution with grain, halation and the film's own colour.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?
No. FilmRue has no account, no ads and no in-app purchases.

Where are my photos?
FilmRue saves to your Photos library. Photos are processed on your iPhone and never uploaded.

What is HEIF+?
Settings → Quality → HEIF+. FilmRue takes a RAW photo and develops it as film, which keeps more detail in highlights and shadows. It takes a little longer to save.

My photo isn't a Live Photo.
Turn on Live Photo at the top of the camera. If FilmRue can't record the motion for a shot it tells you why on screen.

Can I go back to the original?
Yes. Edits are non-destructive: in the Photos app, open the photo, tap Edit, then Revert.

How do I open FilmRue from the Lock Screen?
Add the FilmRue control to the Lock Screen, Control Center or the Action button.

Are the looks copies of real films?
No. Look names and colour are FilmRue's own, and FilmRue is not affiliated with any film or camera maker. A few looks use open-source colour tables from the spektrafilm project (CC BY-SA 4.0), credited in Settings → Acknowledgements.

常见问题

需要注册账户吗？
不需要。FilmRue 没有账户、广告或 App 内购买。

照片存在哪里？
保存在“照片”图库。照片只在你的 iPhone 上处理，不会上传。

HEIF+ 是什么？
设置 → 画质 → HEIF+。FilmRue 拍摄 RAW 并按胶片冲洗，高光和暗部保留更多细节，保存会稍慢一些。

拍出来不是实况照片？
打开取景器上方的“实况”。如果某张照片没能录下动态，FilmRue 会在屏幕上说明原因。

能恢复原图吗？
可以。编辑不破坏原图：在“照片”App 中打开照片，轻点“编辑”，再选“复原”。

怎样从锁定屏幕打开？
把 FilmRue 控件添加到锁定屏幕、控制中心或操作按钮。

这些风格是复刻真实胶片吗？
不是。风格名称和色彩均为 FilmRue 原创，FilmRue 与任何胶片或相机厂商均无关联。部分风格使用了开源项目 spektrafilm 的色彩查找表（CC BY-SA 4.0），见 设置 → 致谢。
